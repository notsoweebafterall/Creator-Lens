# 🔎 CreatorLens

### AI-powered creator campaign intelligence for fintech brands

CreatorLens is a prototype AI system that helps a brand find and evaluate creators for a campaign.

For the current version, I focused on **fintech campaigns**.

The idea is simple:

> Give the system a campaign brief → let it understand the requirements → investigate suitable creators → check their audience and engagement → evaluate brand safety → return a ranked, evidence-backed shortlist.

---

## What does CreatorLens do?

Imagine a fintech brand says:

> "We want an Instagram awareness campaign in India targeting people aged 18–30."

Instead of manually going through creator data and brand guidelines, CreatorLens turns that brief into a structured campaign and investigates potential creators.

The workflow looks like this:

```text
Campaign Brief
      ↓
Understand the campaign
      ↓
Find suitable creators
      ↓
Calculate engagement
      ↓
Check audience fit
      ↓
Check brand safety
      ↓
Combine the evidence
      ↓
Rank creators
      ↓
Final recommendations
