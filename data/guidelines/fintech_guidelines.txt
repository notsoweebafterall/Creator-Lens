FINTECH BRAND SAFETY GUIDELINES

Financial and fintech campaigns require additional caution.

Avoid creators who:
- Promote guaranteed financial returns
- Encourage reckless or deceptive investment behavior
- Present financial speculation as guaranteed income
- Promote scams or suspicious financial products
- Make unsupported financial claims
- Repeatedly provide misleading financial advice

Prefer creators who:
- Clearly disclose sponsorships
- Present financial information responsibly
- Avoid guaranteed-return language
- Encourage informed decision-making
- Use educational and transparent communication
