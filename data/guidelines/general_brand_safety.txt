GENERAL BRAND SAFETY GUIDELINES

Creators should maintain professional and advertiser-safe public content.

Avoid creators with repeated evidence of:
- Hate speech or harassment
- Discrimination
- Threats or glorification of violence
- Explicit sexual content
- Illegal activity
- Fraud or deceptive behavior
- Repeated serious public controversies

Positive signals include:
- Professional communication
- Transparent sponsorship disclosures
- Educational or informative content
- Consistent audience engagement
- Clear separation between opinions and factual claims
